AcmeHR SAML Transaction

Day 3 shows the login as a small sequence of steps. Later lessons will open each step and show the SAML fields and validation behind it.

1

AcmeHR started the SAML login

The Service Provider sent the browser toward the Identity Provider.

Status: NOT CHECKED

2

The browser returned to AcmeHR

The browser came back to the Service Provider after the Okta sign-in flow.

Status: NOT CHECKED

3

The Service Provider accepted the SAML login

AcmeHR accepted the authentication through the SAML security layer. The detailed checks stay hidden for now and are taught later.

Status: NOT CHECKED

4

AcmeHR created its application session

The learner can now open the protected page.

How to troubleshoot this page
