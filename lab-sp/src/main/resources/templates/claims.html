What this page proves

A claim appearing here has passed through the SAML authentication flow and is being read from the authenticated assertion. The groups section shows all received group values. The role section shows only the values that AcmeHR explicitly maps to application roles.

A missing required claim does not automatically mean authentication failed. A received group also does not automatically become an AcmeHR permission. Those are separate application decisions.
